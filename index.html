Phylo-Movies

Scientific visualization software and bioRxiv preprint.

Animates phylogenetic tree sequences from sliding-window analyses to inspect recombination and topological conflict. The project traces back to the University of Vienna thesis "Sequential animation of phylogenetic trees."
